UCI Heart Disease Dataset
Heart disease diagnosis data from 4 medical institutions worldwide.

About Dataset
Heart Disease Dataset — UCI Machine Learning Repository
This dataset contains clinical and diagnostic data collected from patients
across 4 major medical institutions worldwide. It is one of the most widely
used datasets in medical machine learning research for predicting the
presence or absence of heart disease.
The data was originally collected by Andras Janosi, William Steinbrunn,
Matthias Pfisterer, and Robert Detrano in 1988 and donated to the UCI
Machine Learning Repository.
Files Included:

heart_disease_cleveland.csv — 303 rows, 14 features (most popular version)
heart_disease_combined.csv — 920 rows, 15 features (all 4 sources merged)
Data Sources:
Cleveland Clinic Foundation, USA
Hungarian Institute of Cardiology, Budapest
V.A. Medical Center, Long Beach, California
University Hospital, Zurich, Switzerland
Task:
Binary Classification — predict whether a patient has heart disease (1)
or not (0) based on 14 clinical features such as age, cholesterol,
resting blood pressure, chest pain type, and more.
Key Facts:
14 clinical features used for prediction
Target variable binarized (0 = No Disease, 1 = Disease)
Original target had 5 classes (0–4), simplified for modeling
Some missing values present in slope, ca, and thal columns
Ideal For:
Beginners learning classification algorithms
Medical AI and healthcare analytics research
Feature importance and explainability studies
Comparing models across multiple data sources
Citation:
